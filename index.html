04 / Story

Mexico has more than one flavor.

Most canned tequila drinks keep recycling the same ideas.
Lime. Mango. Grapefruit. More sugar.

Mexico gave the world tequila, yet the canned cocktail aisle stopped exploring. Tequila Town starts where the safe flavors end.

Scroll →

Chapter 01 / The Problem

Everything tasted the same.

The tequila was hidden. The fruit tasted artificial. The finish was either too sweet or completely forgettable.

We knew the can could do more.

Chapter 02 / The Search

We went past the obvious.

We looked to Mexican fruit, market drinks, regional ingredients, herbs, flowers, salt, heat, and the flavors people remember long after the glass is empty.

Not exotic for attention.
Different for a reason.

Chapter 03 / The Tequila

Start with the real thing.

We partnered with tequila makers who understand the spirit before it ever reaches the can.

Because sparkling tequila should still taste like tequila.

Chapter 04 / The Flavors

Build every can around contrast.

  • Fruit needs acid.
  • Sweetness needs restraint.
  • Heat needs timing.
  • Tequila needs room to speak.

That balance became the first four flavors.

Chapter 05 / Tequila Town

Made to leave the shelf.

Tequila Town was not created to sit quietly in a cooler.

It was created for festivals, rooftops, beaches, backyards, late nights, new cities, and whatever happens next.

Open one and go.
